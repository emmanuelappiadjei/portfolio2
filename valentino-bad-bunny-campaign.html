Born from

Santo Sol is a speculative genderless fragrance collaboration between Bad Bunny and Valentino — pairing Valentino's luxury identity with Bad Bunny's bold style and Puerto Rican pride.

The scent blends citrus, florals, sea salt, amber, and warm woods to evoke the island's sunlight, coastline, and nightlife, positioning fragrance as an expression of identity and confidence rather than a gendered category.

Campaign line: "Born from the heat. Worn without rules."
